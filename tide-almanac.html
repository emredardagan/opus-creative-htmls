Reading the table

Coefficient

The Breton habit of grading each tide from 20 to 120. Around 45 is a quiet neap; anything above 95 is a big spring tide that uncovers sandbars and pulls the sea far back from the quays.

Spring & neap

When sun and moon line up at new and full moon, their pulls add together and the range swells. At the quarters they work against each other and the water barely bothers to move.

Age of the tide

The biggest tides arrive a day or two after the full or new moon, not on it. The ocean needs time to answer the call. The model here builds in that delay of about 36 hours.
