Why the UV lampFluorite gave its name to fluorescence. Under longwave ultraviolet, traces of europium in the crystal glow violet-blue while everything else in the drawer goes dark.
Rainbow bismuthThe colours are not pigment. A film of oxide only a few hundred nanometres thick interferes with light, and its thickness decides the hue.
Stepped edgesBismuth and some pyrite grow as hopper crystals. Edges grow faster than faces, leaving hollow staircases behind.
