Shellburg

Our town lives on the back of Old Mossback, a very large and very patient sea turtle. She's swimming to the Great Reef, 90 leagues away. You're the new mayor. Please don't upset her.

Build on her shell plates: pick a building below, then click a plate. Click a building to upgrade, repair or remove it.
Decide: every day a citizen brings you a choice. Every choice has a price.
Balance: people need kelp, housing and joy. Mossback needs love and a light load. Happy turtles swim faster.
